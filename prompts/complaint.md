# Prompt: Civil Complaint Draft (`complaint.md`)

## System Prompt Blueprint
Generate a formal Civil Complaint draft under Karnataka Civil Rules of Practice.

### Required Placeholders:
- `[COURT_NAME]`: Principal Civil Judge Court at District
- `[PLAINTIFF_NAME]`: Full Complainant Name & Address
- `[DEFENDANT_NAME]`: Opposite Party Name & Address
- `[CAUSE_OF_ACTION]`: Date & Incident details
- `[PRAYER]`: Specific financial or performance relief prayed for
