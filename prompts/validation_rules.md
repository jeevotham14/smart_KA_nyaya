# Smart Karnataka Nyaya — Document Validation Rules

## MANDATORY FORMALITIES:
1. **Cause Title**: Must contain Court/Authority Name, District in UPPERCASE, Case Type, and Case Number placeholder.
2. **Party Array**: Plaintiff/Petitioner vs Defendant/Respondent with full name, age, father's name, and address.
3. **Pleadings Paragraphs**: Numbered sequentially (1, 2, 3...) containing Jurisdiction, Cause of Action, Statement of Facts, and Legal Grounds.
4. **Prayer Clause**: Clearly demarcated with sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (c) specifying exact prayers sought.
5. **Execution & Verification**: Solemn verification under oath signed by Deponent/Complainant.
